What you’ll build

The trainer above learns your hand in seconds because it borrows a network already trained on millions of photos. That trick is called transfer learning.

Today you’ll build that kind of model yourself, in code, and understand every step: how a network learns, how it sees, and how to reuse what it already knows. Then you’ll play rock, paper, scissors against it.
