What you’ll build

Have you tried Teachable Machine? Show it rock, paper and scissors on your webcam, press Train, and a minute later it recognises your hand.

Today you’ll build that kind of model yourself, in code, and understand every step: how a network learns, how it sees, and transfer learning, the trick that lets it learn from a few photos. Then you’ll play rock, paper, scissors against it.
